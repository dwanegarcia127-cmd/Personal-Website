01 / THE PATH I'VE CHOSEN

Learning to love where I am.

Plans can change. Sometimes, the path we didn't expect becomes the one we want to follow.

At first, I imagined myself taking Computer Engineering because I was interested in both hardware and software. I even considered shifting courses.

But as I continued studying BS Computer Science at Laguna State Polytechnic University, I began to appreciate the course more. Every activity, project, and new concept gave me another reason to keep going.

Our adviser, Sir Al John Villareal, reminded us to stay strong and push through the challenges. That advice has stayed with me. Now, I want to finish the degree I've started while still exploring the other areas of technology that interest me.

A REMINDER I KEEP
