MY STORY

Hello, I'm Dwane Garcia.

I'm currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science at Laguna State Polytechnic University - San Pablo City Campus. As someone who's still beginning my journey in computing, I'm continuously learning new things and discovering what this field has to offer.

My interest in technology comes from my curiosity about how computers work, especially the connection between hardware and software. I became even more interested in technology when I saw a project made by my friend using an Arduino Uno. I was amazed by their project, and it inspired me to hope that someday, I can also use Arduino and create a project like that on my own.

Because of that experience, I became more motivated to explore computer components, understand how technology works, and learn skills that can help me grow in this field. Although I still have a lot to learn, I'm eager to develop my knowledge, improve my abilities, and face new challenges along the way.

Outside academics, I believe there's more to life than studying and working toward a career. I value the time I spend with my family and friends, enjoy exploring my personal interests, and appreciate the experiences that help me grow as a person. As I continue my college journey, I hope to become more knowledgeable, confident, and capable of achieving my goals.
